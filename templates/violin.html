Violin Range Information

This implementation uses technique variation to optimize the available samples:

C2-B2: Spiccato (bounced bow) - sharper, more articulate attack. Using C3-B3 samples.

C3-B5: Regular bowed violin - the primary comfortable playing range

C6-C7: Pizzicato (plucked) - percussive, lighter character. Using C5-C6 samples.

This approach maintains natural sound characteristics while extending playable range through technique variation.
