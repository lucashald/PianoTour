Cello Range Information

Notes above C5: To maintain the natural cello range while providing dynamic variation, notes C5 and above use specialized techniques:

C5-B5: Spiccato (bounced bow) - sharper, more articulate attack

C6-C7: Pizzicato (plucked) - percussive, lighter character

These samples are pitched versions of C3-C4 to create textural variety rather than extended range.
